# Working here

Do the work here. Before the first edit, put it on its own branch, `feat/<unit>`
or `fix/<bug>`, or on a worktree when another session works in this checkout.
Say which in one line.

Reach for `dev-skills:grill` when you cannot say what done looks like; for
`dev-skills:prototype` when the question is visual; for `dev-skills:plan` when
the work will leave this context; for `dev-skills:epic` when it is more than one
unit; for `dev-skills:bug` when a symptom arrives.

Commit per step, green. Drive what you changed before you call it done, looks
included, on captures.

Ask at four points and nowhere else: a grill decision; the three lines under a
new brief; a question the brief and epic do not answer; the merge, which is a
typed `/finish`.
